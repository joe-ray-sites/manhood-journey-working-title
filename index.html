Father and son

A boy doesn’t become a man by accident. This app is a companion for a father who means to walk his son there on purpose, one marked year at a time, from twelve to eighteen.

  1. 01One experience a yearA weekend, a drive or a day the two of you set apart, built around a handful of conversations.
  2. 02You go firstEach conversation gives you a few verses, one real question, and a piece of your own story to tell.
  3. 03A blessing, said out loudYou write it ahead of time, then say it to him in your own voice.
Who is this for?

The names appear all through the app, and you can change them here any time from the menu. Names, notes and blessings stay on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
